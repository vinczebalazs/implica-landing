WORLD PUBLISHED 3H AGO

A China-built VW just skipped the EU's EV tariffs

Brussels exempted the Cupra Tavascan on the condition it never undercuts European prices.

The European Commission exempted Volkswagen's China-built Cupra Tavascan from the extra 20.7% tariff the EU placed on Chinese-made EVs in 2024 — the first exemption of its kind. In exchange, VW agreed to a minimum price, an import cap, and EU investment commitments.

WHY IT MATTERS

It's the first crack in the EU's tariff wall, and Chinese automakers — who don't have VW's home-continent standing — are already asking Brussels for the same deal.

GOOD TO KNOW

The tariff had been steep enough to nearly wipe out the profits of VW's entire SEAT/Cupra division the year before — over one car.
