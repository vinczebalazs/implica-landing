Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Implica ("the Service") is operated by Fly Straight AI Limited, a company registered in the Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC) ("we", "us", or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal data when you use Implica, whether via our website or future mobile applications.

1. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • the public Implica website, which is currently free and read-only
  • future Implica mobile or web applications, where users may register and create profiles

2. Information We Collect

a) Information you provide

Website (current)
You can use the Implica website without creating an account.

If you contact us directly (e.g. by email), we may collect:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • the content of your message

App & Registered Users (future)
When registration becomes available, we may collect:

  • name or display name
  • email address
  • account credentials
  • optional profile information (if provided)

You will always know what information is required at the time of registration.

b) Information collected automatically

When you use Implica, we may automatically collect limited technical data, such as:

  • IP address
  • device and browser type
  • operating system
  • pages viewed and interactions
  • approximate location (country or region level)

This data is collected via logs, cookies, and analytics tools.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal data to:

  • operate and maintain the Service
  • provide access to registered user features (when available)
  • personalize and improve content and user experience
  • communicate with registered users about the Service
  • respond to inquiries or rights-holder requests
  • ensure security and prevent misuse
  • comply with legal obligations

We do not sell personal data.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the EU or UK, we process personal data based on:

  • Legitimate interests (operating and improving Implica)
  • Contractual necessity (providing registered user accounts)
  • Consent (where required, such as for optional cookies)
  • Legal obligations

5. Cookies & Analytics

Implica may use cookies or similar technologies to:

  • ensure basic site and app functionality
  • analyze aggregated usage patterns
  • improve performance and reliability

Where required by law, you will be asked to consent to non-essential cookies.

You can manage cookies through browser or device settings.

6. AI & Content Processing

Implica uses artificial intelligence to generate summaries and contextual explanations of news.

  • We do not use personal data to train AI models
  • AI-generated content is based on news material and general information, not individual user behavior or profiles
  • User accounts (when available) are not used to personalize AI outputs unless explicitly stated

7. Data Sharing

We may share limited personal data with:

  • hosting and infrastructure providers
  • analytics and monitoring services
  • service providers supporting account functionality (when applicable)

All third parties are required to:

  • process data securely
  • use data only for specified purposes
  • comply with applicable data protection laws

8. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to:

  • provide the Service
  • maintain user accounts (when applicable)
  • comply with legal or regulatory requirements

Account data may be deleted upon request, subject to legal obligations.

9. International Data Transfers

As a DIFC-registered company, your data may be processed outside your country of residence.

Where required, we apply appropriate safeguards in line with:

  • GDPR
  • DIFC Data Protection Law No. 5 of 2020

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • access your personal data
  • correct inaccurate information
  • request deletion of your data
  • restrict or object to processing
  • withdraw consent (where applicable)
  • lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

Requests can be made using the contact details below.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

No system is completely secure, but we continuously work to safeguard information.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

If changes are material, we will provide notice through the Service. Continued use of Implica after updates means you accept the revised policy.
